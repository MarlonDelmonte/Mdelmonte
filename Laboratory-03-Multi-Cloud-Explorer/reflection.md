# Mission Reflection

Among the three cloud platforms, Google Cloud Platform impressed me the most because of its strong support for artificial intelligence, machine learning, and Kubernetes. I also found AWS impressive because it provides a very wide range of services that can be used by both small businesses and large organizations. Azure was also interesting because of its strong connection with Microsoft products and services.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three provide computing, storage, networking, databases, security, and identity services. They also allow organizations to use cloud resources without having to maintain all physical infrastructure themselves. Another similarity is that all three platforms have global infrastructure that can support applications in different locations.

Business requirements are very important when selecting a cloud platform. A company should consider its budget, existing technology, application requirements, location, scalability, security, and technical skills. For example, a company that already uses Microsoft products may benefit more from Azure, while an AI-focused company may prefer Google Cloud.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant helped me understand that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. The best choice depends on the specific problem that the organization needs to solve. I learned how to compare cloud services and match them with different business requirements.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio also improved after completing this mission. I added a new laboratory folder containing research documents, comparison tables, recommendations, screenshots, and a reflection. Organizing the files in GitHub helped me practice technical documentation and showed me how a cloud computing portfolio can be developed step by step.
